# Public-page writing

Portfolio uses Mico's voice ("I"); ASTA uses the team's ("we"). Name the work and next action. Keep verified details and testimonial quotations. Never invent experience, clients, results, or guarantees. Do not add deliberate mistakes.

Reference: [Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing). Review patterns in context, not as detector guarantees.

## Coverage checklist

- Content: inflated significance, publicity/awards, superficial analysis, promotional/heritage hype, vague associations/attributions, overstated consensus, canned challenges/futures.
- Language: buzzwords, avoided is/has, all three negative-parallelism forms, title-as-entity, triads, forced synonym variation.
- Presentation: title case, bolding, inline-headed lists, duplicate/skipped/excessive/empty headings, emoji, unnecessary tables, inconsistent quotation marks, separators.
- Residue: assistant correspondence, source-availability hedges, instructions/placeholders, cutoffs, knowledge-cutoff/refusal disclaimers, repeated conclusions.
- Integrity: Markdown/wikitext mismatch, asterisks/hashes/links, all vendor citation artifacts, invented categories/templates, broken/unrelated references, DOI/ISBN errors, incomplete book references, tracking parameters, unused citations, stale dates.
- Context: voice/English-variant drift, bias, model differences. Dates, syntax, punctuation, and detection scores alone establish nothing; explain editorial choices.
- Wikipedia-only: comments, formulaic edit summaries (policy/procedure/citation/template/review assurances), AfC submissions, prefilled maintenance templates, canned user pages, permissions gaming. Not applicable to marketing copy.

Preserve functional labels, routes, legal qualifications, and technical terminology. Prefer concrete descriptions over slogans.
